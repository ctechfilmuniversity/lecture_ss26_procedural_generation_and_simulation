#### Project Reflection & Discussion | Lessons Learned
Achieving my planned look was harder than expected, mostly steering the visual look in the direction that I want instead of just creating a pleasing image with randomized parameters. The performance issues that my laptop had slowed down this experimental process a lot which is why I couldn't implement all ideas I had planned (creating trees with a unique shape like a wheeping willow). Also I am not completely content with the lights and colors yet, since they look very generic. During the experimentation and creation of my results I still learned working about Unreal through discovering the new plugins, working with Instances, navigating in Blueprints and using the Movie Sequencer more than before.
What worked well concerning the process was planning low with the main goal to getting to know the procedural vegetation editor and creating different kind of trees. It seemed like not enough in the beginning but through trying out, failing at some points and the technical limitations it filled the requirements timewise.
